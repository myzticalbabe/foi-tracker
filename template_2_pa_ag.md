# Template 2 — PA Attorney General (Criminal Law Division / Public Corruption)

**Jurisdiction (verified, attorneygeneral.gov Criminal Law Division):** OAG investigates/
prosecutes crimes re the public duties of **state officials and employees**, corrupt
organizations, agency-enforcement referrals, Statewide Investigating Grand Jury
presentments, and Medicaid fraud. The **Public Corruption Section** handles conflicts of
interest, corrupt influence, theft offenses, money laundering — often from referrals by
the State Ethics Commission, Department of State, Auditor General, or a DA with a
conflict or lacking resources.

**Routing — pick the right door before drafting:**
1. County/local matter, no state official involved → **District Attorney first**
   (OAG's own FAQ says the DA oversees the county).
2. Executive-branch fraud/waste/abuse or benefits fraud → **Office of State Inspector
   General**: online form, 1-855-FRAUD-PA, or mail 555 Walnut St 7th Fl, Harrisburg PA 17101.
3. State official/employee corruption → OAG Criminal Law Division / Public Corruption
   Section via attorneygeneral.gov/submit-a-complaint (online or printed form).
4. Police misconduct → officer's agency first (e.g. PSP Bureau of Integrity), DA for
   local departments; judges → Judicial Conduct Board; attorneys → Disciplinary Board.
