# Template 3 — Legislative Committee Correspondence

**Protocol (verified, multi-state legislature guides + LegalClarity):**
- ONE topic per letter — staff file by topic; extra topics are disregarded. Separate
  letters for separate issues.
- Address block: "The Honorable [Full Name], Chairman/Chairwoman, Committee on [Name]".
  Salutation: "Dear Senator/Representative [Last Name]:" — never "To Whom It May Concern".
- Top of letter: your full name, **home address** (proves constituency — without it staff
  may set it aside), phone, email.
- If about legislation: bill number + title in subject line, support/oppose in the first
  two sentences, personal/community impact, concise (one page), research attached not pasted.
- Urge, don't demand. Thank them; invite contact; request a reply if wanted.
- Delivery: postal signed letter (handwritten signature signals authenticity), email copy
  to all committee members + committee staff, or the committee's online testimony portal
  (many states accept written testimony up to 24h after a hearing starts — check the
  specific committee's rules).

**Testifying:** contact the committee administrator 1–2 days ahead to get on the agenda;
open "Mr./Madam Chair and members of the committee"; state name/address/organization and
position up front; route everything through the chair; stay within time (3–5 min typical);
give staff written copies before speaking.

## Packet sections
- [ ] Correct chair + committee name + chamber verified against the current session roster
- [ ] Home address on letter (constituency proof)
- [ ] Single-topic letter with bill number (if any) + explicit ask in paragraph one
- [ ] Supporting exhibits attached as research annex, cited by number
- [ ] Approval gate + `mark-filed` with channel (certified-mail no. / portal receipt /
      email message-ids to members+staff)

**Sources:** WA Legislature testify guide; UT/CT/MN hearing protocols; LegalClarity
legislator-letter guide; ACEP state-advocacy correspondence rules.
